Capstone Thesis · Team of 5

Transformer-based framework that restores images degraded by rain, haze and motion blur together, trained on synthetic data and tested on real-world images.

Diabetes Prediction Using ML & DL

Research Paper · Team of 5

Comparative study on two datasets: clinical measurements (768 records) and early-stage symptoms (520 records).

  • Preprocessing: median replacement, label encoding, feature scaling; six models compared on accuracy, precision, recall and F1
  • Random Forest was best: 99.04% (symptom data) and 77.92% (clinical data)
